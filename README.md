# Mentor Vassals (CK3 Mod)
Mentor Vassals adds right-click character interactions that let you personally re-educate an adult child, vassal, or your primary heir.

The interaction upgrades the target's education trait by exactly one tier (for example, Martial 2 -> Martial 3), with strict limits for balance.

## Features
- Right-click interaction in character interactions: **Re-Educate Subject**
- Target can be:
  - An adult child
  - A vassal (or below)
- Target must share your education track.
- Target must be lower than you in that same track.
- Upgrade is exactly one tier per use
- Per-target cooldown: **3 years**
- Prestige cost scales by your highest title tier:
  - Baron/Count: 200
  - Duke: 400
  - King: 600
  - Emperor: 800
  - Hegemony (AUH): 1000
- Optional flavor rewards included:
  - Small opinion bonus toward you
  - Lifestyle XP in the matching track

- Additional right-click interaction: **Re-Educate Primary Heir**
- Target must be your adult primary heir.
- All normal re-education requirements still apply:
  - Same education track
  - Heir must be lower than your current tier
  - 3-year cooldown on the target
- This interaction catches your heir up to your current tier in a single use.
- Prestige cost is **1000 x highest held title level**:
  - Baron/Count: 1000
  - Duke: 2000
  - King: 3000
  - Emperor: 4000
  - Hegemony (AUH): 5000


## How To Use In-Game
1. Play as an adult landed ruler.
2. Right-click an adult child or vassal.
3. Open the vassal interaction group.
4. Select **Re-Educate Subject**.
5. Pay prestige and apply the education increase.

### Primary Heir Catch-Up
1. Right-click your adult primary heir.
2. Open the vassal interaction group.
3. Select **Re-Educate Primary Heir**.
4. Pay the heir interaction prestige cost.
5. The heir is raised to your current education tier (if all requirements are met).

If a target is not selectable, they are failing one of the scripted checks (cooldown, track mismatch, not lower tier, invalid relation, etc.).
